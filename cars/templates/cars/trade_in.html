How the estimate is worked out

The estimate is a guide, not a promise. It starts from what the vehicle would have cost new, adjusts for the brand and model, then takes off for age, mileage and condition:

value = new price × brand factor × model factor × age factor × mileage factor × condition factor

A real appraisal always depends on the condition of the particular car, its service history and what we have in stock on the day. Bring the vehicle in and we will confirm the number in person.
